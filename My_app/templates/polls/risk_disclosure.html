Risk Disclosure Statement

Important information about the risks of trading

HIGH RISK WARNING

Trading financial instruments on margin carries a high level of risk and may not be suitable for all investors. The high degree of leverage can work against you as well as for you. Before deciding to trade, you should carefully consider your investment objectives, level of experience, and risk appetite.

This document provides important information about the risks associated with trading financial instruments through TradeMaster Pro. Trading involves substantial risk of loss.

Market Risk

Financial markets are volatile and prices can fluctuate rapidly. These fluctuations may result in losses that exceed your initial investment. Factors that may affect market prices include, but are not limited to:

  • Changes in supply and demand
  • Government policies and economic indicators
  • Global political and economic events
  • Market sentiment and investor psychology
  • Natural disasters and unforeseen events

Leverage Risk

The use of leverage magnifies both gains and losses. Even small market movements can have a large impact on your trading account when leverage is used. You may lose more than your initial deposit.

Instrument Maximum Leverage Margin Requirement Risk Level
Forex (Major Pairs) 30:1 3.33% High
Forex (Minor Pairs) 20:1 5% High
Indices 20:1 5% Very High
Commodities 10:1 10% Very High
Cryptocurrencies 2:1 50% Extreme

Liquidity Risk

Some financial instruments may become illiquid, meaning you may not be able to buy or sell the instrument at the desired price. This can occur during periods of high volatility or low trading volume.

Technology Risk

Online trading depends on the reliable operation of technology, including trading platforms, internet connectivity, and electronic systems. Technical failures, system outages, or cyber attacks may prevent you from executing trades or accessing your account.

Regulatory Risk

Changes in laws, regulations, or policies in any relevant jurisdiction may affect the value of your investments or your ability to trade certain instruments.

Recommendation

We strongly recommend that you:

  • Only trade with money you can afford to lose
  • Educate yourself about financial markets and trading strategies
  • Use risk management tools like stop-loss orders
  • Start with a demo account to practice without risk
  • Consider seeking advice from a qualified financial advisor
