Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BoldTrader Pro collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our trading platform.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you:

  • Create an account on our trading platform
  • Make trades or transactions
  • Use our trading tools and analytics
  • Contact our customer support
  • Participate in surveys or promotions

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our trading services
  • Process transactions and send related information
  • Send technical notices, updates, and security alerts
  • Respond to your comments and questions
  • Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes encryption of sensitive data, secure socket layer (SSL) technology, and regular security audits.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which we collected it, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. Trading records are retained for a minimum of 5 years as required by financial regulations.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access and receive a copy of your personal information
  • Rectify any inaccurate personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Object to or restrict processing of your personal information
  • Data portability

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@brandboldtrading.com.
